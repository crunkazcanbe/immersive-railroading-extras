# IR Extras sound credits

Every IR Extras sound is generated by tools in this repo. No third-party recordings are used.

## Synthesised (our own, same licence as the mod)
- `tools/gen_soundpack.py` makes `assets/irextras/sounds/pack/*.ogg`, about 470 files from 125 events. It uses physical models: struck modes, contact bounce, magnetostriction hum, motor slot harmonics, turbulent air, bell partials and reed horns.
- `tools/gen_sounds.py` and `tools/gen_sounds_plant.py` make `assets/irextras/sounds/*.ogg` (the older one-file sounds).

## Station announcer voice (`assets/irextras/sounds/voice/<voice>/*.ogg`)
Each word and phrase was spoken by [Piper TTS](https://github.com/OHF-Voice/piper1-gpl) (`tools/gen_voice.py`) and then band-shaped like a PA horn. Piper is GPL-3. That licence covers the program only, not the audio it produces.

| voice | model | model licence | training data | data licence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `cori` (British female, default) | [en_GB-cori-high](https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/tree/main/en/en_GB/cori/high) | MIT ([rhasspy/piper-voices](https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices)) | LibriVox recordings, see [model card](https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/blob/main/en/en_GB/cori/high/MODEL_CARD) | public domain |
| `lj` (American female) | [en_US-ljspeech-high](https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/tree/main/en/en_US/ljspeech/high) | MIT ([rhasspy/piper-voices](https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices)) | [LJ Speech Dataset](https://keithito.com/LJ-Speech-Dataset/) | public domain |

To add the words of your own station names, run:
`uv run --with piper-tts --with nbtlib python3 tools/gen_voice.py --names "Foo Junction,Bar Road"`
and then `python3 tools/gen_soundpack.py --merge`. Without `--names`, the script reads the station names from local saves.
